Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original human expedition shuttle concept for the game Stellar Drift.
Primary request: a distinctly CLEAN MODERN premium spacecraft design with an immediately recognizable silhouette, broad uninterrupted surfaces and a very small number of functional details. This is a new alternative to overly busy industrial spacecraft. Make elegance of proportion and surface design the main achievement.
Subject: MANTA, a compact 19-metre two-person atmosphere-capable exploration shuttle with a broad low lifting-body silhouette. One continuous shallow swept trapezoidal hull, a gently chamfered blunt forward nose and short thick integrated shoulders. The central walk-in cabin is a clean raised volume flowing into the hull, with one precise narrow dark forward window band. The hull has real thickness for cabin and a small cargo bay. Two narrow recessed engine outlets are integrated into the rear trailing face, no external engine nacelles. One discreet closed rear access ramp. Three simple sturdy landing supports with broad pads.
Design language: only three or four strongly composed primary masses. Approximately eighty percent of the visible skin is calm, smooth panel area. Warm off-white ceramic/composite upper shell, dark graphite underside and glass, one thin muted orange accent line. Clean restrained functional cyan light only at the engine outlet. Carefully sculpted fillets and chamfers, deliberate transitions between surfaces, beautiful controlled highlights. A credible expensive manufactured expedition craft with the design clarity of premium modern science-fiction vehicles. Original intellectual property; do not reproduce any existing franchise ship.
Composition: exactly ONE entire spacecraft, front three-quarter view from a slightly raised camera, full craft and landing gear completely inside the frame with generous breathing room. A tiny single standing 1.8-metre technician beside it gives scale and does not compete. Light neutral blue-gray seamless studio floor and backdrop, soft daylight and controlled shadows. Wide landscape image, crisp realistic high-end product visualization, physically believable materials. No cinematic scene clutter.
Avoid: greebles, clusters of mechanical bits, bolt patterns, little maintenance panels everywhere, exposed pipes, cables, trusses, dangling equipment, decorative radiator combs, huge separate thrusters, machine guns, aggressive fighter styling, heavy weathering, rusty edges, scratches, dirt, chipped paint, noisy textures, complex articulated armor petals, toy or low-poly appearance.
Text: NONE. No titles, numbers, labels, logos, slogans, watermarks, borders, inset views, diagrams or interface.
