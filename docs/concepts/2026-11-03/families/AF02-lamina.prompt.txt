Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium AAA photoreal original alien spacecraft FAMILY CONCEPT SHEET for an original science-fiction game.
Composition: ONE high-resolution LANDSCAPE sheet with exactly FOUR clearly DIFFERENT complete alien spacecraft, individually separated in an orderly TWO-BY-TWO layout, all fully inside their own quadrant, generous gaps and outside margins. No overlaps, cropping or extra ships. One consistent elevated front three-quarter product-view camera, neutral gray-blue studio background and softly filled daylight studio illumination. Each ship is individually scaled for shape comparison, NOT shown at a common physical scale. No grid lines or sheet borders.
Class positions, for design planning only and NEVER written on the image: upper left = probe / scout; upper right = patrol craft; lower left = worker / collector; lower right = command / heavy craft. Four classes must differ substantially in proportions, mass arrangement and silhouette, NOT simply be bigger copies of one hull. Shared family logic but clearly different builds and functions. Make the worker's working/capture volume and the heavy's larger load-bearing organization visually understandable without conventional human equipment.
Quality and detail discipline: premium photoreal aerospace industrial concept rendering, carefully developed large structural volumes, real depth and thickness, controlled surface curvature, clean well-maintained materials, a small number of purposeful large seams and functional interfaces. Excellent silhouette readability and quiet expensive engineering. Neither a low-poly toy nor noisy space junk. No grime, battle damage, scratches, exposed bundles of cable, small greebles or arbitrary decorative clutter.
Universal constraints: alien technological ships, not human planes or boats. NO human cockpit, canopy, windows, wings, missiles, gun barrels, conventional rocket nozzles or ordinary jet engines. No recognizable existing science-fiction franchise copies, no EVE ship copies, no Star Wars TIE scheme. No text of any kind, NO letters, numbers, labels, titles, captions, logos, watermark, insets, schematic drawings or humanoid scale figures. Exactly FOUR distinct ships on the sheet.
School: LAMINA — tall thin separated SHEAR PLANES with finite structural thickness. Slanted slab faces, offset layers, vertical sails and staggered fan constructions. Panels are dark matte muted JADE ceramic with GRAPHITE edges and a very small amount of icy pale-blue functional light. Broad clean planar faces, carefully designed chamfers and edge thickness, minimal seams. Alien precision, no human aircraft anatomy.
Upper-left scout: a narrow asymmetrical set of THREE short staggered upright fins, attached along an offset slender lower beam, forming a broken zigzag silhouette. No symmetrical pair of wings. Thin planes but real edge depth.
Upper-right patrol: a TALL single dominant slanted vertical shear slab with a second shorter offset plane and a third small counterbalancing plate connected through a stepped side spine. It reads as a sculpted vertical sail, not an airplane or triangle. No centre ball or cockpit.
Lower-left worker: a LOW broad fan or rack of FOUR unequal short shear plates tilted at different angles around an open working cradle; a compact offset rectangular hub connects them at one side. The entire silhouette is horizontal and asymmetrical, distinctly unlike the tall patrol.
Lower-right command/heavy: a deep offset stack of FIVE expansive oblique planes, with varied lengths, large intentional gaps and stout short ceramic bridges joining them along one angular flank. A monumental layered cantilever with an off-centre mass axis; no orb and no two-sided symmetry.
Structural rule: planes appear thin relative to their span, but must have real beveled thickness and credible continuous connections. Clean plate surfaces and a small number of large junctions. Some top, side and end faces visible under the same lighting, to make orientation and material unambiguous.
Critical: NOT a central sphere between two panels, NOT a TIE fighter, NOT an ordinary triangular wedge, NOT conventional wings around a fuselage. No closed rings, open horseshoes, stone ornaments, solar-cell patterns or machinery noise. Four strongly different proportions and mass layouts.
